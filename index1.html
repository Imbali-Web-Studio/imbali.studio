Our Process

1.

Consultation

An initial 15-45 minute kick off call to discover your business needs and target improvements.

2.

Layout Selection

You will be provided with 2-3 layouts specific to your needs and aligned with your brand.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a project actually take?

Most Foundation builds take 2–3 weeks from kickoff, Growth runs 3–4 weeks, and Authority projects typically take 4–6 weeks depending on scope. Timelines depend on how quickly content and feedback come back — faster turnaround on your end keeps the project moving.

What if I need more pages or features later?

You're never locked in. Every package can grow with you — additional pages, integrations, or copywriting are priced as simple add-ons, so scaling up later is straightforward and never a renegotiation.

Do I own my website after it's built?

Yes. Once your project is paid in full, you own the design and content outright. Imbali retains rights to pre-existing frameworks and reusable systems used to build it, but your site is yours.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes a window of post-launch revisions. After that, ongoing care plans are available for businesses who want updates and support handled for them on an ongoing basis.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short discovery call, your brand assets (logo, photos, existing copy if you have it), and a deposit to secure your project slot. From there, the process in the section above takes over.
