About Us

Imbali Studio

IMBALI Studio is a mobile-first digital studio focused on designing responsive website solutions and automated systems for ambitious brands.

IMBALI was created to inspire entrepreneurs and business owners to level up their online presence and take charge of their digital footprint through structured, functional website solutions that don’t just serve as a brochure, but function as a true funnel, naturally improving the flow of your business.

Every site is thoughtfully customized, not just with a logo and colors, but in layout and flow, to reflect your brand and how you operate. This work isn’t transactional. It's a commitment to helping entrepreneurs and business owners operate at their highest level.

How It Works

Our Process

1.

Consultation

An initial 15-45 minute kick off call to discover your business needs and target improvements.

2.

Layout Selection

You will be provided with 2-3 layouts specific to your needs and aligned with your brand.

3.

Content Exchange

Once you select your layout, we’ll discuss the content needed to tailor your website to YOU.

4.

Buildout

You'll receive your first mockup, up to 48 hours after your content is received. Two revision sessions are included.

5.

Testing

During this phase your website will be tested for broken links, responsiveness, speed and basic SEO.

6.

Website Launch

Congratulations! Your new website is fast, mobile friendly and functions with the way you do business.
